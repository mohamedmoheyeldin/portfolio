## The eight topics

1. **Vibe:** precise, current, credible; a premium product page for an engineer. References: the architecture diagram on a well-made developer platform's home page; an instrument panel at rest; a clean systems review.
2. **Journey:** who he is, with the system he works on already running beside it; then six projects as evidence; then the career to scale; then what he brings; then the next step.
3. **Energy:** a strong, alive open, a dense and quick middle, a steady career, a practical capabilities act, a confident close.
4. **Feeling and the one moment:** oriented, curious, convinced, grounded, equipped, invited. The moment: the signal reaches the three role nodes and the picker opens the first part, so the visitor sees a real system before reading a word about it.
5. **One thing no site does:** the hero is the documented system itself, running and explorable, not a picture of the person.
6. **Range:** premium technical minimalism with one light source; dark is the richer theme, light is just as designed. Dot grids only under diagrams, one glow, one colour.
7. **One world or scenes:** distinct scenes sharing one surface language (tiles, hairlines, dot-grid figure grounds).
8. **Assets:** none photographic (repo rule: no logos, no branded imagery, no mock interfaces). Diagrams are SVG and HTML drawn from the record. Credential badges on Experience only.

## Grammar: system schematic (new)

The page opens on a working diagram beside the claim, then lays out the evidence as a grid of figure tiles, a scale bar and a capability grid, and closes on a coloured band. Nothing is pinned; the one device is the diagram, which holds attention by running and answering, not by holding the scroll.

**Forbids:** a pinned or sticky stack; scrub video or full-bleed media; a command palette; a dark plate or a sheet as the close; the name at poster or composition scale; chapter numbers or a folio; scroll-jacking; checklist ticks as you read.

Why the others lost: filmic one-shot and continuous world need footage or geography; rhythmic cutlist is the wrong energy for hiring evidence; gallery, chaptered editorial, typographic poster, live surface, split stage, change review and case deck are taken (FINGERPRINTS rows 1 to 7).

## Signature move: the schematic

A working diagram of the documented VA Claims Workflows design (six parts from `flows.ts`): a signal runs along its paths, and a picker under it shows one part at a time and lights it, by click, keyboard focus or pointing at the diagram; it steps through the parts twice on its own when motion is welcome and stops once touched. Bespoke to this build: no prior row put an explorable system in the hero.
