# Design brief: the signal

The site is a precise product surface built around a working diagram. The first screen pairs the claim with a live schematic of the documented VA claim-status design: health-provider APIs feed the claims platform, which reaches VA staff, providers and veterans, with the claims framework, Tableau dashboards and Playwright checks alongside. A signal runs along its paths, and every part can be picked to read what it does. The work follows as a grid where each project carries its own figure, the career sits on a bar drawn to scale, and every page closes on a teal band. The identity is a cool near-white or near-black ground, white or graphite tiles with hairline borders, one signal colour (teal), Instrument Sans and Geist Mono. It replaced the casebook build (a stacking case deck) on October 30, 2026; the full Scrollcraft brief is in [SCROLLCRAFT-BRIEF.md](SCROLLCRAFT-BRIEF.md).

- **Hero.** Open-to-roles status, then the `h1`: the name as a line and the claim at display scale ("I work with the people who use the software, build what they need, and test it before they do."). Below it, the current role and the eleven years in one sentence, Email me, the resume popover, LinkedIn and GitHub, and a row of three results (3 user roles, Under 5 min, 17 fields) that jump to their tiles. On the right, the schematic.
- **Signature move: the schematic.** An SVG of the six documented parts of VA Claims Workflows (`src/content/flows.ts`), in a wide layout beside the copy and a tall layout on phones. The list under it carries every part as text; `enhance.ts` turns it into a picker: one part at a time, its text shown in one place, the part lit in the diagram. Clicking, focusing with the keyboard or pointing at the diagram picks a part. Where motion is welcome, a signal runs into the platform and out to the three roles, and the picker steps through the parts twice on its own, then stops; it stops for good as soon as the visitor touches it. Without JavaScript every part's text is listed. The caption says it is a diagram of the documented design, not a capture.
- **Work.** Six tiles on a grid (on wide screens: the first two across the top, the rest in pairs). Each tile: number, period and kind, the name linking to the case study, the one-line description, a figure restating its documented result (the three roles with live status; the five-step run; seventeen field cells then rules, a person and the outbox; one record to website, PDF, Word and text; four check types on a GitHub Actions rail; four browsers and the release path from Dev to Staging), the result with its evidence tag, the source link and Case study. Figures are decorative; the result text carries the facts.
- **Career.** A tinted band with every role on one bar drawn to scale from September 2015 (widths computed at build time from the record), year ticks below it, then each role under it with its dates, title and compact highlights. The current role is teal.
- **What I bring.** Six numbered capabilities restating documented highlights, the tools by group, and the credential and education line.
- **Navigation.** A full-width top bar: a small teal mark, the name and role, Work, Experience, Contact (the section in view is marked on the home page), the theme switch and Email me. It is clear over the hero, gains a blurred ground once the page moves, and a thin teal line along its foot fills as the page is read. Below 860px the routes move into a native popover menu that also lists every resume file.
- **Close.** A teal band on every page: "Next step", the question, the address set in mono with a copy button, Email me (inverted) and the resume popover; LinkedIn, GitHub and all four resume files beside it; the two-week AI workflow audit offer across the foot. The band rescopes the tokens locally for light type on teal.
- **Type.** Instrument Sans (variable weight and width; display set at 92% width with tight tracking) and Geist Mono (labels, numbers, dates, the address). Two families, self-hosted Latin subsets with metric-matched fallbacks.
- **Colour.** Light: ground `#f6f7f7`, tiles `#ffffff`, ink `#0b0f0e`, teal `#0a6b5d`, band `#0a5c50`. Dark: ground `#07090a`, tiles `#111516`, ink `#eef3f2`, teal `#46d4b8`, band `#0e3a33`. A soft teal glow lights the hero from behind the diagram; diagrams and figures sit on a fine dot grid. Green and red appear only in self-test results. Every ink token clears 4.5:1 on every ground in both themes (the self-test measures it).
- **Motion.** Native CSS only, inside `prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference`; scroll-driven parts also inside `@supports (animation-timeline: view())`. The hero copy arrives in reading order and the schematic settles beside it; the signal runs twelve times and stops; the status dot and the mark pulse three times; section heads, tiles, capabilities, roles and the close rise as they enter; figure bars, the career bar and the timeline draw; the top bar gains its ground and fills its reading line with the scroll. The picker's colour changes ease only when motion is welcome. Transform, opacity, colour and shadow only; no scroll-jacking, no smooth scrolling.
- **Content rules.** Career facts come from `career.json`; no invented numbers, logos, generated imagery or mock interfaces. Diagrams restate documented fields and say so. No em dashes in visible copy.
