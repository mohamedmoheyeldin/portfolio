# Design brief: the thread

The site is a run of full-bleed acts that alternate a near-black ground and a warm bone ground, with headlines in capitals at poster scale and one coral string drawn down the whole page as it is read. The user asked for the bold look of dakshgajjar.com (an Awwwards portfolio): this build takes that genre (alternating grounds, poster capitals, a single line through the page, objects over giant words) and fills it only with this record's facts; no text, drawing or code was copied. It replaced the signal build on October 30, 2026 (on the `newAI` branch); the full Scrollcraft brief is in [SCROLLCRAFT-BRIEF.md](SCROLLCRAFT-BRIEF.md).

- **Intro.** On a first visit with motion welcome, the signature (Mohamed Moheyeldin in a fast hand: oversized looping capitals, quick lowercase, a slash and an oval exit flourish, from the public-domain Hershey script) writes itself on near-black with a ring on the pen tip in about 2.8 seconds, then lifts away to the hero. Skipped by any key, click or scroll; once per visit.
- **The string.** One coral line over the whole home page, drawn as it scrolls with a ring on its tip: through the claim, down the right margin, around each heading block in a rounded rectangle, through each scene word, and into the close as a knot that pulls into a check mark while it crosses the screen.
- **Hero (dark).** Open-to-roles status, then the claim filling the screen: "I build what users need and test it before they do." Under it, in mono, the name, current role, employer and the eleven years, with Email me, the resume popover, LinkedIn and GitHub. The string starts under the bar and loops through the claim.
- **Statement (bone).** "From the people who use it to the system behind it." with one mono line restating the documented sectors and years.
- **The system (dark).** The schematic of the documented VA Claims Workflows design (six parts from `flows.ts`) on a lit stage: it assembles as it scrolls in, pulses run along its paths with the scroll, the chosen part glows, and the parts are tabs whose current tab fills a progress line while it steps on its own; the caption says it is a diagram, not a capture.
- **Work.** A heading act ("Six projects. Every result sourced."), then one act per project, alternating grounds: number, period and kind; the real name and description; a descriptive word set to the full width (Claims, Generator, Documents, Resumes, Checks, Releases; labels, not product names); bone paper cards over the word restating the documented result (the three roles with live status; the five-step run with its output and checksum manifest; seventeen field cells, rules and the person review; one record to four outputs; four check types on GitHub Actions; four browsers and the release path); then the result, evidence, source and Case study.
- **Career (dark).** "11 years. Three teams." and every role on one bar drawn to scale, with compact highlights.
- **What I bring (cream).** Six numbered capabilities in two groups (forward deployed; engineering and quality) either side of a coral arc with a node level with each, the tools by group, credentials and education.
- **Close (dark).** "Have a workflow your users are fighting with?" at poster scale, the address with copy, Email me, the resume popover, routes, all four files, the audit offer. The string arrives here as a knot and pulls into a check mark.
- **Navigation.** A dark bar on every page: the name in capitals, the role in mono, Work, Experience, Contact in mono capitals, the theme switch and Email me, with a coral reading line. Below 860px a native popover menu.
- **Type.** Instrument Sans at 700 in capitals with tight tracking for posters; Geist Mono for labels, ledes and paper cards.
- **Colour.** The Claude palette (the user's choice): cream `#f0eee6`, ink `#141413`, coral `#d97757` for the string, buttons and live marks, clay `#9c4128` for accent text on cream. In the dark theme the cream acts are dimmed to `#d8d5ca` so the alternation holds. Every ink token clears 4.5:1 on every ground (the self-test measures the page ground; axe checks every act).
- **Motion.** Native CSS only, inside `prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference`; scroll-driven parts also inside `@supports (animation-timeline: view())`. The claim arrives a line at a time; the diagram assembles and the capability arc draws; poster lines rise; scene words drift against the scroll; paper cards drop onto them; the career bar and timelines draw. With reduced motion everything is shown at rest, the string drawn in full; the intro and the string tip are skipped.
- **Content rules.** Career facts come from `career.json`; no invented numbers, logos, generated imagery or mock interfaces. Papers restate documented fields. No em dashes in visible copy.
