# Design brief: the diff

The site shows what changed for the people using the work. Each featured project is set as a **workflow diff**: the documented "before" lines against the documented "after" lines, the way an engineer reads a code change. The identity is graphite and warm paper with one signal colour, vermilion, and type that does the heavy lifting. It replaced the split (People / Systems) build on October 30, 2026; the full Scrollcraft brief is in [SCROLLCRAFT-BRIEF.md](SCROLLCRAFT-BRIEF.md).

- **Hero.** Open-to-roles status, the name at display scale (Instrument Sans, condensed width, tight tracking) as the `h1`, then the claim: "Forward Deployed Engineer. I sit with the people who use the software, build what they need, and test it before they do." Email me, the resume popover and See the work; a facts row (now, experience, domains, LinkedIn and GitHub); and the working loop, five stations on one rail (Listen, Define, Build, Verify, Demo), each restating a documented highlight. On load a vermilion signal runs the rail twice and stops.
- **Signature move: the workflow diff.** `src/content/diffs.ts` holds before/after pairs for every project, each line restating `challenge`, `outcome`, `approach` or `metric` (sources commented per pair) with a note on where the evidence comes from. The diff is complete without JavaScript; with it, a Before / Diff / After control filters the lines and pointing at a line outlines the line it replaces. As the diff crosses the screen the removed lines arrive first, then their replacements.
- **Navigation.** A sticky translucent bar (name and role, Work, Experience, Contact, Jump to, theme, Email me) and a command palette (Ctrl K or ⌘K, or "/") that searches every section, project, role, skill group, resume file and contact action, built from the career record at build time. Phones get the palette and a popover menu.
- **Work.** A project index (number, name and context, the result, the evidence source), then three featured projects: head, the result with its evidence tag, the diff, the problem and my part, the documented design in a disclosure, tools and links. Phones read result, diff, then detail. Three compact rows follow.
- **Career.** A timeline with dates in mono on the left of the rail and the current role marked in vermilion. **How I work:** four practices that restate documented highlights, then the tools by group and the credential line.
- **Close.** A dark contact plate on every page and in both themes (the plate rescopes the colour tokens locally): the question, Email me, the resume popover, the address with Copy, LinkedIn, GitHub and all four resume files. The two-week AI workflow audit offer sits under it.
- **Type.** Instrument Sans (text and display, variable weight and width) and Geist Mono (labels, data, the diff chrome). Two families, self-hosted, with metric-matched fallbacks.
- **Colour.** Light: paper `#f6f5f1`, ink `#141413`, vermilion `#b5360f`. Dark: graphite `#0d0d0e`, ink `#f2efe9`, vermilion `#ff7a4d`. Green and red appear only where they mean something: diff lines and self-test results. Every ink token clears 4.5:1 on every ground in both themes (the self-test measures it).
- **Depth and surfaces.** Hairlines and space group content; panels only for objects (the loop, the diff, the toolkit groups, the contact plate, credential cards, fact sheets). Three warm-tinted elevations and an edge light. A fine engineering grid fades in behind the hero and the plate.
- **Motion.** Native CSS only, inside `prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference`; scroll-driven parts also inside `@supports (animation-timeline: view())`. Hero load in reading order; the loop rail draws and the signal runs twice; sections rise as they enter; diff lines arrive in order; the career rail draws down; the header gains depth after the first scroll. Transform, opacity and a background-position band only; no pinning, no scroll-jacking, no smooth scrolling.
- **Content rules.** Career facts come from `career.json`; no invented numbers, logos, generated imagery or mock interfaces. Diffs and diagrams restate documented fields and say so. No em dashes in visible copy.
