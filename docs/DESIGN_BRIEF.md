# Design brief: the split

The site is held on two sides for its whole length. **People** (left, warm paper): who the work was for, what they needed, how I worked with them. **Systems** (right, a cool blue-tinted ground): what was built, how it works, how it was checked. A 1px seam joins them from the header to the close. The positioning line already has two halves, "Built with the people who use it. Tested before they do.", and the two halves of the profile (forward deployed engineering and quality engineering) map onto them. The full Scrollcraft brief is in [SCROLLCRAFT-BRIEF.md](SCROLLCRAFT-BRIEF.md).

- **Hero.** The name at composition scale crosses the seam: one person on both sides of the table. Under it, the two halves side by side, each with its headline. People holds Email me, the resume menu and the route to the work; Systems holds the self-test.
- **Signature move: Test this page.** Sixteen real checks run against the rendered page in the visitor's browser and report pass, fail or not measurable, with the measured value. Nothing is precomputed. It demonstrates the habit the page claims instead of describing it.
- **Projects told from both sides.** Featured projects: context, title, description, who it was for, the problem and my part on the left; the system diagram, the result with its evidence source, tools and links on the right. The other three are compact rows on the same split. On phones the result leads the Systems band.
- **Career on the seam.** Each role's dates sit on the seam as a mark; who it served on the left, what I built and checked on the right.
- **The close collapses the split.** As the contact section scrolls in, the Systems ground clips away to the right edge and the seam goes with it, so People holds the full width for "Email me". With reduced motion it rests collapsed.
- **Type.** Instrument Sans (variable weight and width) for text and display, Geist Mono for labels, dates and values. Two families. Display tracking tightens with size; dark mode sets body one step heavier and looser.
- **Colour.** One blue in two lightnesses, keyed to the theme. Light: paper `#f6f4ef` beside cool `#edf1f8`, ink `#15171c`, accent `#2445c8`. Dark: warm charcoal `#131312` beside blue-black `#0d1220`, ink `#eeeff1`, accent `#8aa4ff`. Every ink token clears 4.5:1 on every ground in both themes (the self-test measures it).
- **Depth and surfaces.** Hairlines and space group content; panels only where something is an object (self-test, result, diagram steps, credential cards, audit offer). Three elevations, tinted to the ground, plus a 1px edge light.
- **Motion.** Native CSS only. On load each hero half arrives from its own side. Diagram lines draw down as they cross the screen, rows rise in, year marks settle onto the seam, and the close collapses. All behind `prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference`, scroll-driven parts behind `@supports (animation-timeline: view())`; transform, opacity and clip-path only; no pinning, no scroll-jacking, no scroll cues, no smooth scrolling.
- **Content rules.** Career facts come from `career.json`; no invented numbers, logos, generated imagery or mock interfaces. Diagrams restate documented fields and say so. No em dashes in visible copy.
